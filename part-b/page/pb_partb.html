Part B · The prototype

Honest Search.

A working search box on this page, running on the real 568-deal catalogue and the real 8,997-row log. Type anything into it. Every answer is rendered twice — OLD, replaying what the log actually recorded for that query in that city, and NEW, what a search that reads intent, checks the city's real stock and says what it just did would return. It is a working prototype with the explanations built around it, not a finished interface for an end user.

01

Pick a kind of query, or type your own. Eleven chips, grouped under the same three problems as Part A. Whatever you type, the chip for its kind lights up — so a typed query always says which of the three it belongs to.

02

Read the line between the panes. One of eight answers, always: matched, translated, corrected, category only, thin, not sold here, above ceiling, not understood. The NEW pane opens by naming what changed against OLD.

03

Change the city — or pick “All markets”. Twenty cities with their real deal counts. The same query answers differently in London and in Kraków; that is the point, not a bug. “All markets” checks the whole catalogue in one step.

04

Open “Show more information”. The Part A finding behind that answer, what today does, what this does instead, whether search can fix it at all — and proof that every one of the 8,997 logged searches gets one of the eight answers.
